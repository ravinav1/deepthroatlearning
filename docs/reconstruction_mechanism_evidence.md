## Mechanisms already tested

### Generator ordering and tie behavior

**Hypothesis and source.** Supplied labels support a strong Vinje G1≥G2≥G3 preference:99.9112% of all case-hours,99.9641% in2021 and99.9364% in2022. This descriptive search used all2,922 labeled cases, including the later development years. Supplied topology shows distinct penstock groups, so units are not freely exchangeable. The ordering is an empirical tendency, not a derived physical law.

**Test and alternative.** A32-choice2021 search over smoothing/projection selected no smoothing plus L2 isotonic projection of Vinje probabilities. AUC changed0.98065317→0.98069869; the frozen2022 choice changed0.98435803→0.98438025. Every nonzero smoothing candidate lost on2021. Full Tokke ordering was not imposed: G1/G3/G4/G2 has genuine exceptions and different shared-penstock interactions. [Original observations and search](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/modeling_recommendations.md).

**Remaining uncertainty.** A later, nondeployable permutation oracle on historical public predictions and supplied pre2021 labels could remove2,418/20,379 Tokke errors and150/32,866 Vinje errors. It suggests allocation/ties explain some discrepancies, not all. It does not establish a usable permutation rule or the simulator's exact tie-breaking perturbation. [Prior external research](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/historical_solver_mechanism_research.md).

### Startup and shutdown economics

**Supplied formulation.** Fractional commitment satisfies Pmin·u≤P≤Pmax·u and Qmin·u≤Q≤Qmax·u. Supplied start/stop costs multiply positive/negative state changes. Boundaries are free because initial commitment is not supplied; no preceding label is invented. Public SHOP concepts informed the formulation, while cost values and operating bounds are supplied.

**Measured effect.** On the fixed36, linked states with zero cost changed Q-AUC0.9253279764→0.9257442937; adding supplied transition costs reached0.9446521745 and reduced switch-count MAE4.1865→0.9187. This separates economics from the float32-RHS correction. It was uneven: Tokke_G3 AUC declined0.00175822, and six units acquired more threshold errors. [Audited four-arm result](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/lp_shop_relaxation_result_audit.md).

**Learned confirmation.** The separate startup142 feature model selected25% into the clean canonical115 anchor:2021 AUC0.9952596524→0.9953329489 and unchanged2022 0.9940509265→0.9940802862. Haukeli improved in both years; Kjela's2021 gain reversed in2022. The final CSV and full inference replay exist; its [branch record](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided_startup142_result.md) does not claim a leaderboard result. Exact initial state, integer starts and solver tie handling remain unresolved.

### Shared conduit losses, cross-flow derivatives and busbar approximation

**Mechanism.** Supplied loss coefficients imply head loss k·Q², coupling every unit on a shared conduit. An own-unit conditional curve misses the other units' power derivative. The implemented cross-flow correction uses supplied efficiency curves and a declared ±0.001m finite difference; it changes only the objective. SINTEF's2019 energy-conversion presentation and earlier archived reference comparisons inspired this investigation. The alternative busbar model uses supplied gross curves and aggregate cubic losses, approximated by eight convex intervals with an explicit negative-price approximation. Neither is a proven copy of SHOP17.17. [Derivation and source](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided-only-crossflow-gradient.md), [busbar recipe/result](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided_busbar144_result.md).

**Evidence and falsification.** On the additional24, cross-flow Q-AUC was0.950371153 versus matched bounded control0.945004823, but threshold errors were5,323 versus5,320. The learned gradient144 blend improved2021 to0.9953035361 then failed2022 at0.9940156939 versus anchor0.9940509265. Conditional144 also failed its frozen2022 check. More physical-merit iterations did not help: the fixed12 original gradient Q-AUC0.96799410 exceeded both best-step iteration5 0.96577902 and iteration20 0.96577726. Mixtures preserve linear water constraints, not certified nonlinear PQ feasibility; interpolated duals are not valid shadows.

Busbar's36-case Q-AUC was0.954244763; additional24 0.951771834 versus baseline0.942608774. Its10% learned correction narrowly passed2021/2022 at0.995280404/0.994058533. Haukeli and Lio improved in both years, while Kjela declined in both; Hogga declined in2022. The audited final CSV exists. These small learned gains do not establish faithful simulator reconstruction or explain the external0.99952 result. ### Reservoir pooling and tunnel pressure

**Causal correction.** The original Vinje pool merges Vaamar/Totak/Venemo/Hyljelihyl. The tested split preserves their supplied stores/routes and constrains signed transfers using supplied volume–head tables, openings and tunnel loss coefficients. Pressure-flow magnitude is √(|Δhead|/k) m³/s with directional wet/dry branches;0.0036 converts it to hourly Mm³. Public tunnel semantics and historical case1561 comparisons motivated the mechanism; no external flow or multiplier enters the candidate. The remaining Kjela aggregate routing approximation is documented.

The static initial-head cap raised case1561 Haukeli generation from zero in the free split to577.326MWh and separated upstream/downstream water shadows. Haukeli's12-case Q-AUC improved0.823069→0.889137, but total AUC fell0.949887→0.943897. Cases2023/2121 explained most of that loss: their supplied-storage head changes made fixed caps stale. This falsifies a fixed-envelope full-cache remedy. At the same initial reference, the earlier storage-revenue derivative actually **increased** the Haukeli proposal205.038→210.271MWh; lower final output came from different accepted paths, not an established derivative-sign bug. [Mechanism and quantitative checks](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided-only-haukeli-tunnel-mechanism.md).

**Completed equality test.** Beginning/end-hour signed-pressure affine updates, five undamped steps each, reached Q-AUC0.958419545/0.958932636 versus original0.949926896. However, final maximum true flux residuals were897.624/349.764m³/s with1,056/1,050 wrong-sign tunnel-hours. Improved label ranking therefore did not demonstrate hydraulic feasibility. Input-only decomposition attributes most error to the unrestricted square-root tangent crossing its reference sign, not mainly head interpolation or the derivative cap. Tunnel timing remains unresolved; the separately observed beginning-hour generation-head convention is not proof of tunnel timing. [Equality result and mathematical review](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided_vinje_tunnel_equality_review.md).

### Integrality and active operating curves

**Tested hypothesis.** Active-domain curves use Q=Qmin·u+ΣΔQ and P=P(Qmin)·u+Σslope·ΔQ with binary commitment and ordered segments at nonpositive prices. Bounds, efficiencies and costs are supplied; public full-MIP comparisons helped motivate integer investigation. The origin-inclusive relaxation is a real approximation, but correcting it need not reproduce solver-selected labels.

On the separate proven-control12 population, the three-binary active-domain test increased all-unit errors2,643→2,657; the all14-binary test increased them to2,682 and reduced Q-AUC0.94220059→0.92913186. All14 improved Tokke_G2 errors321→293 and Vinje_G3 476→436, but worsened Tokke_G1 77→108 and Byrte20→70. Only2/12 all14 solves proved optimal within the10-second cap. The busbar all14-binary12 test also lost against continuous busbar. These are bounded surrogate results, not proof that integrality is irrelevant or that the proprietary nonlinear optimum was solved. Exact working-point segmentation, mixed/full-MIP schedules, solver tolerances, startup boundary defaults and tie rules remain open. [Active-domain record](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided_active_domain_result.md), [generation-convention research](../reports/reconstruction_20261103/research/provided-only-generator-conventions.md).
