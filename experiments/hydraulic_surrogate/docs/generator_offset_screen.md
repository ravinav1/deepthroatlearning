# Bounded generator offset objective screen

This tests whether direct pooled pair optimization learns useful small shifts between generators. It uses already projected, genuinely forward predicted single608 LightGBM probabilities. It is a development mechanism screen, not a claim about the accepted GPU ensemble or a new independent holdout.

The earlier Round9 calibration fit one positive common logit slope and 14 generator offsets with ridge BCE (C=0.001); its 2021 second-half pooled AUC fell by 0.000483283508. A positive common slope is ranking-equivalent to scaled offsets, so this is not a new ranking function family. The distinct comparison is fixed uniform pooled pair loss versus matched globally balanced BCE, with only 12 bounded offsets and a shared Vinje offset. The recent feature-dependent tree residual also failed; this experiment cannot alter within-unit ordering and therefore isolates between-unit ranking.

Both arms fit 12 group biases from zero, bounded to [-0.5, 0.5]. Vinje G1/G2/G3 share one bias. First apply the frozen Vinje projection; then transform probabilities by the exact odds-scaling formula. Zero bias preserves original bytes, endpoints remain endpoints, and actual strict ranks and ties are checked for every unit. Both objectives include 0.5 * 0.01 * mean(bias²), with gradient 0.01*bias/12 and Hessian 0.01*I/12. There is no sweep or retry.

The pairwise arm samples one fixed pool of 1,000,000 positive and negative endpoints uniformly and independently with replacement, RNG seed4173, and minimizes mean log(1+exp(score_negative-score_positive)). Same-group pairs are retained. The control minimizes mean globally class-balanced BCE using weights N/(2*N_class) over all training output rows. Both use deterministic L-BFGS-B, maxiter500, maxls40, ftol1e-14 and gtol1e-9. Successful optimization and maximum projected gradient <=1e-7 are required. Exact full Hessians include cross-group pair terms. Synthetic finite differences check both full gradients/Hessians and penalty scaling; fits and pair draws are replayed exactly.

Both stages require pairwise pooled AUC strictly above identity and the matched balanced BCE arm, plus above identity separately in each prespecified calendar half. Per-unit AUCs must be exactly unchanged, actual ranks/ties and Vinje order must be preserved, and both fit replays must match. Failure stops the branch; no refit on failed screen targets or automatic promotion. Passing2020 would justify a separately frozen chronological experiment on the accepted ensemble, not immediate submission.
