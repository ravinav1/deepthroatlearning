The joint cache and trainer integration pass the accounting, provenance and information-availability checks below. This establishes consistency of the stated approximation, not equivalence to the unknown SHOP case-generation commands. No frozen source or cache was changed.

## Physical and economic accounting

The one-hour conversion is `volume_Mm3 = discharge_m3s * 0.0036`. Production uses `0.00981 * discharge_m3s * effective_head_m * turbine_efficiency * generator_efficiency`, in MW; over one hour its numeric value is MWh. Consequently, price in EUR/MWh times hourly production is EUR, and terminal EUR/Mm3 times storage is EUR.

The audit independently accumulates every turbine, bypass and spill edge into each pool's hourly balance, preserving separate bypass destinations. Maximum reconstructed reservoir-balance error in the eight cases is 3.87e-12 Mm3. Physical storage remains nonnegative. Reconstructed revenue plus terminal value minus release costs and soft penalties agrees with the stored objective to at most 2.57e-6 currency units. These checks include the final hour's inflow and final storage.

Terminal values retain the baseline conversion: local end-of-horizon EUR/MWh multiplied by the best initial-head unit production ratio in MWh/Mm3, plus downstream global value. This matches the structure documented for `energy_value_input`; it is not an upstream-minus-downstream subtraction of raw EUR/MWh. The initial-head curve/grid and choice of pooled reservoir remain approximations. The actual CSV-to-SHOP attribute assignment was not supplied. [SHOP water values](https://docs.shop.sintef.energy/examples/water_values/water_values.html)

With balance orientation `v[t]-v[t-1]+out-in=natural` and a minimization solver, physical marginal water value is the negative equality multiplier after restoring the objective scale. Exported economic margins divide the upstream-minus-downstream water shadow by a standalone best-energy factor. That factor is a comparative coordinate, not the derivative of joint plant production. [SciPy HiGHS marginal convention](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.linprog-highs.html)

The supplied bypass/spill costs are used numerically as the same currency as price. SHOP's attribute tables label some costs NOK while the dataset's market inputs are EUR. No currency command or conversion is available; this is an explicit modeling assumption. The audit verifies dimensional consistency conditional on that assumption, not an exchange-rate claim. Future startup experiments must preserve this distinction. [Generator cost attributes](https://docs.shop.sintef.energy/objects/generator/generator.html#startcost-const)

## Convex allocation and negative prices

All saved vertices in the sampled cases reconstruct exactly from legal initial inputs and frozen code. Their shared losses use total main-tunnel flow and the assigned penstock's combined flow. The same convex weights reconstruct each unit's flow, power and on-fraction; generator constraints satisfy `Pmin * on_fraction <= power <= Pmax * on_fraction`. Weight mass is at most one, unused mass is off, and the old Tokke/Vinje independent discharge is exactly zero. Network routing is therefore counted once. [SHOP plant loss attributes](https://docs.shop.sintef.energy/objects/plant/plant.html#main-loss)

Keeping both Q/P hull boundaries permits physical lower-production vertices under negative prices. The sampled negative-price cases did not actually select those lower vertices; the separate forced-release synthetic test demonstrates that choice. Other seven plants still use the explicitly documented baseline constant-conversion approximation in negative-price hours. Neither part is claimed to recreate every nonlinear hourly operation.

The two-dimensional plant hull preserves the finite-grid optimization objective when only aggregate flow and production matter. It does not preserve every possible unit allocation. Equal Q/P points or interior points with different unit masks may be removed, so unit-level features retain allocation ambiguity. This is a limitation of the current surrogate, not label leakage.

## Inputs, chronological split and provenance

The LP case reader requests only `Run No` and `starttime` from the commitment file. A guarded reader verified this restriction; an added poisoned label field did not enter case construction. Replacing every reservoir-volume observation except the legal case-start row with NaN in memory leaves every case parameter and joint vertex unchanged. Raw files were untouched. Prices, inflows and constraints over the supplied 168-hour horizon and the supplied terminal water values are optimizer inputs, so their future timestamps are allowed. No commitment labels or future realized volumes enter the solver.

The trainer's normalized joint path constructs base, physical and case-local price-context features, then the joint cache, then normalization. It does not also append the baseline LP cache. The running manifest reports exactly 612,696 rows and 404 columns. All 87 `lpjoint_*` columns are dimensionless and are preserved by normalization. The audit checks the trainer's complete joint source/data/cache digest, column and mathematics spec against the frozen manifest.

Chronological training ends on 2020-12-25 for the 2021 fold and 2021-12-25 for the 2022 fold, so all 168 training-label hours finish before the next validation year. Validation cases are IDs 2193–2557 and 2558–2922 respectively. The fixed 40% training-hour sample does not affect this exclusion. Micro ROC AUC flattens the aligned hour-by-unit arrays; this audit did not re-score supervised predictions. The independent saved-array selection track owns that check.

All seven cache source hashes and all raw-data hashes match. The feature archive digest is `5fd1de4d08bf5db95892dc66a870431f38c3571e1bcc65ec62cbbe8882eb5db6`. Source closure contains the builder, joint implementation and imported feature/head/shared-loss helpers, but not unrelated trainer/export code. Replay additionally compares the full feature specification and checkpoint hashes. Relocating a recipe requires explicit path mapping because the stored cache path is absolute; weakening checksum/spec verification is not warranted.

One defensive API limitation should be improved in a new version: the cache loader verifies its own case IDs/dates plus the input frame's length and repeated horizon-hour sequence, but receives no case-ID vector for the input frame. A caller could reorder entire 168-row blocks without that sequence changing. The audited trainer constructs its frame in the required order and applies no sampling/reordering until after loading, so this does not invalidate the current run. A future public loader should require an explicit ordered case-ID argument or compare a deterministic legal-input fingerprint. Do not alter the frozen interface/cache to make that improvement silently.

## Highest-value next physical experiment

The next bounded experiment is jointly optimized fractional startup/shutdown costs on Tokke/Vinje, using per-subset hulls. SHOP supports fractional startup and shutdown costs outside MIP, but their activation depends on commands/plant flags. The supplied topology omits the case command sequence and initial generator states. This supports an explicit experimental relaxation, not a claim that the competition used it. [SHOP linear startup](https://docs.shop.sintef.energy/commands/set_linear_startup.html)

Crucially, startup costs cannot simply be attached to the existing global two-dimensional hull. An interior point with a different active subset may avoid an expensive transition. The new formulation must preserve upper/lower hulls separately for each subset, define each unit's on-fraction exactly from vertex weights, and charge positive/negative differences between adjacent hours. No zero-flow point may carry an active-unit mask. Initial and terminal transitions remain uncharged because their states are unknown. This rules out a free independent variable that stays on while generating no power.

The predeclared comparison uses the same fixed 32 cases and grid, with three reported alternatives: frozen global hull, per-subset hull without switching cost, and per-subset hull with the supplied costs. All results, including negative or weaker on-fraction diagnostics, must be retained. Head trajectories, terminal-value conversion, other plants and labels remain unchanged. A full cache requires separate review. Combining directed Haukeli storage with joint plant features is also physically justified, but corrects a distinct mechanism and should remain a separate ablation.
