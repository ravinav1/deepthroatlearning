# Physical mechanism screens

These small pre-2021 diagnostics investigate missing physical mechanisms. They do not measure leaderboard performance, and they do not promote a new model. The existing GPU664 experiment separately tests the already audited 56 continuous SHOP features.

## Head-dependent turbine flow bound: do not expand

`head-flow-bound-screen-001` inspected 36 metadata-selected cases and all 14 units without decoding targets or solving a model. The suspected omitted constraint was turbine maximum flow interpolated at net head. Every one of the 504 case-unit combinations already respected that explicit turbine bound.

The largest old-versus-physical capacity ratio was 1.0000362606, a difference of 0.003626%. No ratio exceeded 1.01. The largest absolute flow-capacity discrepancy was approximately 0.0041633 cubic metres per second. These tiny discrepancies arise from the independently interpolated maximum-power roots, not a missing turbine-flow limit.

The preserved raw manifest says `screen_passed: true`: its predeclared alternative gate counted any dispatched flow more than 0.000001 cubic metres per second above the independently reconstructed maximum. That tolerance flagged 12.02% of old LP hours and 11.34% of SHOP hours despite the negligible capacity difference. This is a false positive for the intended mechanism. The gate is not rewritten after observing the result; the interpretation explicitly rejects expansion. No new LP cache or fit is justified by this check.

The exact command, source and input hashes, environment, 504 measurements and 1.95-second runtime are preserved under `artifacts/head-flow-bound-screen-001/`. An initial environment-only source inspection using system Python failed because PyYAML was unavailable; the existing project environment succeeded. That failure did not produce a solver or model result.

## Integer schedules coupled to the full water budget

The earlier nonlinear dynamic-programming surrogate used network LP shadow prices as fixed costs, so it did not enforce the finite weekly water budget jointly with its integer unit schedule. The continuous SHOP model retained the network budget but relaxed every unit state. Making only the existing Hogga, Lio and Haukeli state vectors binary isolates that remaining distinction without adding unsupported minimum run times, ramp limits, new costs or fitted thresholds.

`integer-water-budget-screen-001` used the frozen original network, supplied costs and 12 metadata-selected pre-2021 cases. The original exact relaxed controls came from the independently verified full cache. Each new MILP received an 8-second solver limit followed by two identical 1-second conditional LP replay limits. Labels were loaded only after all physical results were saved. The original protocol hash is `9a4f29a1ad78204f391d1a4d4b100e1ea6d67519bdb0d0fa50c00a69d984b60b`.

Eight cases returned proven optima. Four cases, 1826, 1827, 1958 and 2055, returned time-limit status with finite integral incumbents. All 12 fixed-state LP replays were bitwise equal, all four slack types were zero, and no objective exceeded the original continuous relaxation. The diagnostic took 59.01 seconds, with each case below 8.6 seconds of measured wall time.

Descriptively, the three target units' cutoff errors fell from 844 to 688, an 18.48% reduction. All-unit errors fell from 2,805 to 2,597, and pooled discharge-proxy AUC rose from 0.94086722 to 0.94194829. Lio errors fell from 190 to 33, Hogga changed from 318 to 319, and Haukeli remained at 336. All five metric gates passed, but the all-proven gate failed. The original screen therefore remains failed, and these metrics include unproven incumbents.

The root agent subsequently authorized a separately frozen solver-completion continuation, selected solely by the four timeout statuses. It gives those four cases a 60-second MILP limit and reuses the other eight results and proofs byte-for-byte. The mathematical formulation and all acceptance gates remain unchanged. Its protocol hash is `3a71920074eafc262d7842b37fca1c6ea03d8a3cebd0c7821fb7345656b74a47`; its preflight passed all source/data/base-artifact hashes and the exact 8/4 partition. Results are recorded separately under `artifacts/integer-water-budget-completion-001/`. No expansion or promotion is authorized by this continuation alone.

The continuation completed in 69.47 seconds. The four retries took 9.88, 13.00, 21.67 and 23.75 seconds and all returned status 0, zero native relative gap and one branch-and-bound node. The combined twelve-case result passes every original gate: target-unit errors are 701, all-unit errors are 2,643, and pooled discharge-proxy AUC is 0.94219828. Lio errors are 46; Hogga remains 319 and Haukeli remains 336. Compared with the first screen's time-limited incumbents, the proven economic optima slightly worsen cutoff errors while improving pooled proxy AUC; the optimizer's objective is not the target-label metric.

Every native MILP incumbent-minus-dual bound is exactly zero in the saved numerical report. The independently reoptimized conditional LP objective differs from that bound by at most 0.00000294 EUR in either direction, consistent with solver arithmetic. These conditional LP duals remain conditional on the fixed integer paths. The independent audit passed source/data/result hashes, all eight byte-identical reuses, original matrices, saved integer feasibility, labels, metrics and all six gates, without running an optimizer. Its proof is `artifacts/integer-water-budget-completion-independent-audit-001/manifest.json`, SHA `f428c3633d3fe7f5a83dffd06f0c2edf1324443a2cee465dc01052ab7c76b7d2`.

The twelve proven-case times total 92.05 seconds, mean 7.67 and maximum 23.75 seconds. A single full pass extrapolates to 7.77 CPU-hours or 3.89 hours with two workers. Full fresh MILP replays approximately double the work. The original screen stored one conditional LP output while asserting both calls matched; a new metadata-spaced, entirely label-free 24-case timing pilot saves both fixed-state arrays and duals and runs fresh-process MILP replays before any full-cache decision. The proposed exact 664-column prefix plus 84 additional physical features and immutable failure/resume policy are documented in `docs/integer_water_budget_full_cache_proposal.md`.

The broader pilot completed all 48 fresh-process attempts in 137.31 seconds with two one-thread workers. All were optimal; all 24 complete result replays and all 96 saved fixed-state LP outputs/duals matched bitwise. No labels were decoded, no failure fallback was needed, and all slacks were zero. Mean attempt time was 4.65 seconds, 95th percentile 15.07 and maximum 16.33; maximum worker RSS was 0.90 GB. Actual two-worker elapsed throughput extrapolates to 5.80 hours for a complete primary-plus-replay cache, before difficult-case tails or OOF contention. Native relative gaps were all zero; absolute incumbent-bound arithmetic differed by at most 3.35e-8 EUR, while conditional LP objective-bound arithmetic differed by at most 7.01e-6 EUR.

The independent pilot audit verified unique process IDs, exact metadata/input/hash closure, complete replay arrays and the reconstructed primal-dual optimality conditions, without solving or decoding targets. Its proof SHA is `dd8f7372ab8a347f98270b7687489dedd001be6b46ec1aaa838f14fdd9a4e391` at `artifacts/integer-water-budget-pilot-independent-audit-001/manifest.json`. Maximum stationarity residual was 1.16e-11, complementarity zero and primal residual 2.20e-12. This establishes useful reproducibility on the selected sample, not a guarantee about all remaining cases.

The 84-column transform is implemented in `sintef_project/integer_water_budget_features.py`. Focused tests cover transition alignment and physical units, conditional-dual cancellation, rejection of unproven/slack/nonbinary cases, finite dual/price schemas and exact prefix preservation. A refreshed input-only feature preflight computed all 24 × 168 × 84 values from both pilot attempts and found every value equal in literal dtype and bytes. Its manifest SHA is `2c73b44a2132aa2754d724072e648ea6f6a67fdbadac945e7fe2ed406f1f52c8`, at `artifacts/integer-water-budget-feature-preflight-002/manifest.json`.
